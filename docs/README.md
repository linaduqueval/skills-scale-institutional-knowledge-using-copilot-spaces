# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

OctoAcme runs cross-functional projects through a consistent, iterative lifecycle. Work begins with **initiation**, where the team validates the business need, defines measurable success, identifies stakeholders, and records the initial timeline and risks in a Project One-pager. Once approved, **planning** turns the initiative into prioritized backlog items with estimates, acceptance criteria, a Definition of Done, responsibilities, dependencies, and release milestones.

During **execution**, delivery teams work in small, testable increments using a project board and regular ceremonies. The Project Manager coordinates delivery, schedules, risks, dependencies, and communication; the Product Manager defines outcomes and prioritizes the backlog; developers implement and test features; and QA validates quality and acceptance. Daily standups, weekly delivery syncs, demonstrations, and PM–Product Manager alignment provide frequent visibility. Risks and blockers are tracked in a risk register and escalated from team-level triage to the PM, Product Lead, or Sponsor when needed.

Quality is integrated throughout delivery: pull requests include issue links and acceptance criteria, CI runs automated tests and linting, security scans are completed, and team review and approval are required before merging. Testing includes unit, integration, and critical end-to-end smoke tests, with manual QA when appropriate. Releases proceed through staging and production verification, stakeholder communication, and rollback or incident procedures. After sprints, releases, incidents, or major milestones, retrospectives capture lessons and assign owners and due dates to focused improvement actions.
